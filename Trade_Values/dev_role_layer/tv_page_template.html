Dynasty Trade Values

Value over the rest of 2026 and the next five seasons (through 2031). 2027 rookie picks are valued over the same seasons: their first five NFL seasons. It adds a young player's potential from his draft slot, how players at his position age, his chance of staying in the league, and a discount that makes later seasons count a little less.

2027 rookie picks are priced before the 2027 class is drafted. Players in our 2027 mock draft (Renner) use the post-draft model, with the mock pick as their draft capital. Everyone else uses the pre-draft model, which has no draft capital. Each prospect gets an expected value from his chances of each fantasy tier, with what past players in that tier scored, adjusted for his age. Then the prospects are ranked for your league: pick 1.01 is worth the best prospect's value, 1.02 the second best, and so on through round 3. It assumes every prospect on the board enters the draft.

How they're built

Points over replacement, scoring models, caveats and backtests

The values are built from fantasy points per game over replacement: how many more points a player scores each game than the player you could start instead. Who that is depends on your league, which is why the league settings move every value. Because the values are measured in points, they add up the way points do. The top player on each sheet is 100,000. The full explanation is in the guide: redraft, dynasty and picks.

  • 2026 data runs through the latest week shown above. Values will move as the season goes on.
  • Each scoring setting has its own fitted model (credibility, aging, availability and staying in the league), not just rescored points. Lineup settings move the replacement lines.
  • QB catches always score full PPR, and RB or WR passing TDs always score 4 points. Both are too rare to matter. The first-down bonus counts rushing and receiving first downs only, never passing.
  • Expected games use each player's current status: injured reserve, the last injury report (Out, Doubtful, Questionable) and his depth-chart spot, with injury misses kept apart from depth-chart benchings. The tags beside names show the status.
  • Dynasty upside uses the spread of how far past projections actually missed one to five seasons out. In backtests from week 9 of 2016-2020, predicted totals came out at 0.95 to 1.01 times what players actually produced across five league setups. Rookie picks carry one calibration multiplier (1.13, 95% range about 0.80-1.47) measured the same way.
  • Rookie pick values are rougher than player values. A mock pick is a stand-in for the real draft slot, and prospects outside the mock have no draft slot at all, so a highly rated prospect the mock left out can still rank high. Ages are estimates.
  • In backtests on 2016-2025, the redraft values and the dynasty values both ranked what actually happened better than "his current pace continues," for every scoring setting and every league size tested. Details are in the guide.
